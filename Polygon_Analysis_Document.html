📐 Chvátal's Art Gallery Theorem

For any simple polygon with n vertices:
At most ⌊n/3⌋ guards are required and sometimes necessary to see every point in the polygon.
Examples:
n=8: ⌊8/3⌋ = ⌊2.67⌋ = 2 guards max ✓
n=12: ⌊12/3⌋ = ⌊4⌋ = 4 guards max ✓
n=20: ⌊20/3⌋ = ⌊6.67⌋ = 6 guards max ✓
